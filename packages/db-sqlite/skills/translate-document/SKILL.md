---
name: translate-document
version: 1.1.0
description: Translate a complete historical Classical Chinese document into the requested target language (Korean or English) before alignment.
---
Read the entire source and its context before composing a complete translation in the requested target language.
Use confirmed punctuation when supplied; otherwise interpret the unpunctuated source yourself. Never require the user to punctuate or segment first.
Preserve all source meaning, rhetorical relationships, names, dates and qualifications. Do not summarize or silently omit difficult passages.
Organize translation paragraphs by meaning and rhetorical flow, independently of source line breaks. Preserve verse and enumerations when appropriate. Do not force one source sentence into one translated sentence.
Follow the supplied terminology, Hanja annotation, supplementation and uncertainty policies. Distinguish uncertain readings and supplied explanations from source evidence; never invent certainty.
Review the complete translation against the source for omissions, additions and consistency before submitting it.
